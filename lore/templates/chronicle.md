# Chronicle

Copy this file to `lore/campaign/chronicle.md` after a clone.

Condensed progression of this game. What changed. Not a reprint of the archive.

A human uses this as “the story so far.” A cold read uses this before writing the next post or offering Design options.

Default trigger: add a beat after about ten posts, or sooner when a scene closes, a day passes in-world, someone sleeps or goes unconscious, or the System might owe a look. Idle chatter can sit in the archive and earn no beat.

Newest beat at the **top**. A load-bearing beat may run a paragraph or two. Do not write a novel.

Do not copy a person’s `history.md` into this file. This is the shared spine. Their file is how they lived it.

Blank or `unknown` = unknown. Do not invent.

**Link** points at the archive cards or post range. Default: `TBD`.

## Card (copy)

**When:** In-world mark and/or post range. Example: Posts 11–19. Night after the chamber.

**Link:** Vault link to the archive cards or post range. Default: `TBD`.

**Who:** Names that matter to the beat.

**What changed:** What is now true that was not. A paragraph or two is allowed. Not every line of dialogue.

**System:** Only if something printed or was refused. Class, Skill, Condition, level. Detail lives on `history-system.md` / `record.md`. Blank if the Design was silent.

**Why it still matters:** What a later scene must not break. Concrete. Not “heavy.” Blank if the beat is only a cite.

**Additional:** Only what does not fit above. Leave blank if nothing extra is true.

# Faction

One named group. Copy this file to `lore/campaign/factions/<slug>.md` after a clone. One faction per file.

A human uses this as a refresher. A cold read uses it so a town, party, guild, or house is not invented twice.

Membership on a person is a line on their `profile.md`. How a *person* feels about this group is a card on their `relationships.md`. This page is the group itself.

Blank or `unknown` = unknown. Do not invent. Off-screen moves are the narrator.

---

## At a glance

**Name:** What they are called. What the street calls them, if that differs.

**What it is:** Party, town, guild, house, company, Wistram hall, other. One line.

**Where:** Place they are known to occupy. Hall, street, ship, road. Blank if they have no seat.

**Influence:** What they can actually move in play. Coin, blades, votes, fear, a license. Not a score. `unknown` if unestablished.

**Disposition:** How they treat their own and how they treat a stranger. One to four lines.

---

## Purpose

**Stated:** What they say they are for.

**Kept:** What they actually chase, if that differs. Blank if unestablished.

---

## Ethics

What this group will spend. What it will not do as a body. Not each member’s private mind.

---

## Members

People who matter to how the group runs. Not a census. One card each. Link to their person folder when it exists.

In Obsidian, fold this heading. Do not delete folded text.

### Member (copy)

**Person:** Real name, as on their folder. Example: First Last.

**Link:** Vault link to their folder. Default: `TBD`. Replace TBD when the folder exists.

**Position:** What they are in this group. Captain, clerk, patron, the one who actually decides.

**Note:** One to four lines. Function in the group. Not their full profile.

---

## Additional

Only what is unique to this group and not already above. A rule they enforce. A debt the group carries. Leave blank if nothing extra is true.

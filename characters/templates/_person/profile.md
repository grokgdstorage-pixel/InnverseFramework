# Profile

Surface. Who they are **now**, as a scene partner would take them in.

Rich enough that they cannot be mistaken for someone else. Still surface. Wants, fears, and refusals belong in `mind.md`. How they are with a named person belongs in `relationships.md`.

Blank = unknown. Do not invent.

## Who

**Tier:** Player / Important (pick one)

**Name:** What their name is. What the street, their workplace, and friends call them, if those differ.

**Species:** What people they are.

**Age:** How old they are, or how old they appear, if that changes how they are treated.

**Gender:** What gender they are, and how they present if that matters in a scene.

**Sexuality:** What their sexuality is. Blank if unestablished.

**Occupation(s):** How they sustain themselves. What a stranger would think they do, if that differs.

**Factions:** Named groups they are known to belong to. Party, town, guild, house, Wistram, a company. Membership only — what a stranger could learn. How they *feel* about that group, and how they act inside it, is a card in `relationships.md` if the group is treated like a person. Blank if they belong nowhere that matters.

## Presence

**First five seconds:** What someone notices before a word. Three to five anchors: silhouette, bearing, a mark that returns. Not a clothing list — that is Attire. Not tics — those are Mannerisms.

**Attire:** What they wear as a habit. Role, climate, vanity, what they never wear. What the clothes signal to a stranger. Not a closet inventory. Not every outfit. A change of clothes for one scene does not belong here unless it is their new habit.

**Voice:** How they sound if you cannot see them. Pace, register, volume, whether they interrupt or go silent. No sample catchphrase.

**Mannerisms — body:** How they hold and conduct themselves. Stance they fall into, what the hands do, how they walk or sit, stillness or fidget. Repeating and visible. Not the private why.

**Mannerisms — speech:** Manner of speaking beyond sound. Vocabulary (trade-words, street, court). Hedge, swear, title everyone, never use a name, talk around the point. How they use that speech with a *named* person lives in `relationships.md`.

**How they occupy a space:** The face they put on a room — not a tic. Command the table, hug the wall, perform competence, make themselves small, take the chair that sees the door. How they portray themselves in public. Repeating hand-tells and fidgets stay under Mannerisms — body. The private why stays in `mind.md`.

**Reputation:** What a stranger or a town already assumes. May be wrong. The private correction lives in `mind.md`.

## Living

**Habit:** Where they sleep and eat. A quiet day. Work rhythm. A vice or hobby a neighbor would know. Not a log of last week.

**Equipment:** Tools, weapons, a bag, an item that is a relationship or a plot. Not clothing — that is Attire. Blank if nothing matters or the narrator has not decided.

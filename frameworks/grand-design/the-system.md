# The System

## Scope

Parent manual for the System in this kit.

This file answers:

- what the System is
- what it watches and discounts
- how it judges
- how it chooses a product (or silence)
- how far a gift may go
- when the person hears the announcement
- how Class, Skill, and level are one machine

This file does not answer:

- a specific Class change, name, or consolidation
- which Skill to pick inside a pool
- when a level fires, or capstone procedure
- counter-leveling growth rate
- magic, posts, or campaign house rules

Innverse System physics. Plots and characters are non-canon. Campaign exceptions belong in `reference/campaign/`, not here.

Play text is opened with `reference/operations/play-packet.md`. This file does not run that pipeline and does not award XP.

## How to use this file

Read this file before any Class, Skill, or level ruling.

Then open only the child that matches the product chosen:

- Class change, name, hierarchy, or consolidation → `classes.md`
- Skill pick, pool, ceiling, obtained vs learned → `skills.md`
- Whether they step, capstones as a level event, counter-leveling growth → `leveling.md`

If those children are not written yet, stay inside this file. Do not invent a child procedure.

### Output

This block is for a Grok ruling packet. It is not forum-post text and not the in-world announcement.

Return:

1. Product chosen, mix chosen, or silence
2. One short reason (mind, deed, becoming, reach, cost — only what applied)
3. In-ceiling options when a Skill is in play
4. Which child would be opened next, if any

Do not treat a wiki anecdote as a new law.
Grok does not finalize. The narrator does.

### Stops

- Do not invent a floating Skill pool.
- Do not award a Skill past that Class pool’s ceiling (up to 20 levels above that Class’s current height). Return in-ceiling options. Narrator only beyond the ceiling.
- Do not announce except as §5 allows.
- Do not finalize an extreme. Options only, unless a human authorizes the mark.
- Do not rewrite this file for one campaign.

## Core philosophy

The System is a standing judgment over mortal effort. It watches a person’s mind, their deeds, who they are becoming, and what impact their life has on other people and the world around them. It then marks that shape with a Class, a level, a Skill, or silence.

It is not a quest log. It is not a fairness engine. It does not owe a reward because someone suffered, and it does not withhold because they were kind.

| Product | What it marks |
|---|---|
| Class | The role the person is inhabiting or becoming |
| Level | That this Class has grown enough to step |
| Skill | A capability taken from that Class’s pool and handed over now |

One machine. Three products. A ruling that treats them as three games will drift.

## What it watches

- **Mind.** How they see themselves and their path. Self-perception is not flavor text.
- **Deed.** What actually happened — done, failed, or endured — not what was planned or claimed.
- **Becoming.** Who they are turning into over time.
- **Reach.** Impact on other people and on the world around them.
- **Need and desire.** These color a pick from a pool. They do not invent a pool the Class does not have.
- **Cost.** A deed that costs something weighs more than a safe routine.
- **Kind of deed.** What type of thing this was, and which Class would honestly own it.

Identity is how they name themselves and how the world would name them if it were honest. When those disagree, the System sees both. It does not have to pick the flattering one.

## What it discounts

These are not sins. They are reasons the System often stays quiet or stays small.

- **Empty grinding.** Motion that teaches nothing and risks nothing. Practice that sharpens them is not this.
- **Time as a claim.** Years held are not a deed. Years of refinement, dedication, or reach can be.
- **Unearned player want.** A request from outside the character that the person has not moved toward in mind, deed, or becoming.
- **Age as a law.** The System does not tax years lived. Someone old who still holds a real flame is judged by that flame and those deeds.
- **Class-count as a law.** Extra Classes are not a fine. Time is the limit. High drive can move more than one Class because more than one path is actually lived. That costs more in the same hours. The highest levels tend to sit on one poured-in path. High-level multi-Class lives require more capability and drive, not a penalty.
- **Wish without weight.** Wanting a higher-hierarchy Class while thinking and acting as a lower one does not open the higher pool.

## How it behaves

### 1. Watch

Mind, deed, becoming, reach, need, desire, cost, and kind of deed.

### 2. Judge

Has this life, so far, reached a point the System will mark?

Acknowledgement can come from any of these, alone or stacked: change, refinement, practice, dedication, impact, accumulation, reach.

No single verb is required. Busywork still does not qualify.

### 3. Choose a product

The System pays what the culmination is worth.

It might be:

- only a level
- only a Skill (obtained or learned)
- a Class gained
- a Class renamed
- a Class consolidated
- old Skills upgraded
- new Skills gained
- silence

Any combination of the above can occur together when the culmination warrants it.

Tie-break: choose the smallest mark that fits. Mix only when more than one product actually changed.

Do not default to a full bundle. Do not forbid a full bundle when the life has earned it.

How to build each product is the child’s job.

### 4. Fit the gift

Each Class has its own Skill pool. Draws come from that pool.

From that pool the System may pick by deed, need, desire, mind, or a plain draw.

**Hierarchy** is the quality of the pool. Lower hierarchy to higher hierarchy is a progression. The higher Class has more possibilities and more capability.

Flavor or culture can differ without a rank step ([Ninja] / [Assassin]). Rank steps enrich the pool ([Soldier] → [Veteran], [Swordsman] → [Swordmaster], [Lord] → [King]).

**Level** is investment in that pool. A Level 5 [Swordsman] and a Level 30 [Swordsman] share a pool-color. They are child and adult of one line, not two Classes.

**Ceiling (that Class’s pool only)**

- Most Skills sit near the current height in that Class.
- Weaker Skills still happen.
- Stronger Skills happen as luck, need, desire, tragedy, or a capstone.
- Ceiling: a Skill obtained from that pool may sit **up to 20 levels above** that Class’s current height.
- That number is a ceiling, not an average. Most draws sit far under it.
- Past the ceiling: narrator only. Grok returns in-ceiling options and stops.

A Skill obtained or learned without leveling a Class still uses a real pool. When unsure, use the Class they were actually living, not the one they wished. No floating pool. That pool’s ceiling still applies.

Skills already held can grow as the person levels. That is investment, not a new draw.

| Slider | Does | Does not |
|---|---|---|
| Hierarchy | Colors and enriches the pool | Replace level |
| Level | Multiplies what that pool can pay now | Invent another Class’s pool, or jump the ceiling |

### 5. Speak or don’t

Judgment may already have landed while they were awake.

They hear the announcement only when:

1. They are **unconscious**. Usually sleep. This is the ordinary time the System speaks.
2. They are **counter-leveling**. The lower-level side is under odds that should break them. Heard **while awake**.

Counter-leveling is the exception to the sleep rule. It is not a second System. Growth rate under those odds belongs in `leveling.md`.

No voice on an ordinary good day. Conscious and not in that struggle: no announcement, even if the mark already landed.

## How the products interact

- Class chooses the pool and how rich it is.
- Level marks growth in that Class and multiplies what the pool can pay. In this kit a level’s main gift is Skill and the fact of the level, not a stat block.
- A deed feeds the Class that owns that deed.
- Off-level Skills still use a fitting pool and that pool’s ceiling.
- Class changes are identity judgments, not a menu. `classes.md`.
- Usual play order: `leveling.md` (do they step) → `skills.md` (what they receive) → `classes.md` (only if the Class itself changes).
- Magic sits beside the System. Not in this file.

## Examples

Not a catalog. Pattern checks only.

**Silence.** A [Guard] holds the same gate for a month with the same inner story. No sharper practice, no reach, no cost. Nothing.

**Mind without a new title.** Same gate, but they begin to think of it as theirs to keep. The System may mark a [Guard] path more honestly, or wait. It does not hand [Ruler].

**Player want.** They ask for a rare combat Skill after a quiet week. No matching deed, cost, or becoming. Silence or a small in-path mark. Not the requested Skill.

**Skill only.** A season of real practice on the gate. A [Guard] pool Skill, usually near current height, still under the [Guard] ceiling.

**Craft.** A [Potter]’s forms finally change: tighter control, pieces other people seek, a standard they will not drop. No battle. Mark the [Potter] path or stay silent if it was only busy hands. Not a combat Class.

**Level only.** A [Strategist] sees the board better than last time and pays for it. Class name unchanged. A level. Skill pick is `skills.md`.

**Outlier under the ceiling.** A new [Soldier] lives through a night that should have ended them. A [Soldier] pool Skill, possibly one a [Veteran] would want, still under the [Soldier] ceiling. Not a [General] Skill. Not the default.

**Past the ceiling.** Same night, if the fitting Skill would sit past the [Soldier] ceiling: in-ceiling options only. Narrator decides the extreme.

**Same pool, different investment.** Level 5 and Level 30 [Swordsman] share a pool-color. The Level 30 ordinary Skill can match a Level 5 wonder.

**Hierarchy.** [Swordsman] → [Swordmaster] is lower to higher hierarchy. More possibilities and capability. Level still multiplies inside each.

**Mix.** Years of one path, or a crisis that finishes a change of self, may combine consolidation, upgrades, new Skills, and a level. Not the weekly result.

**More than one Class.** If more than one path is lived hard enough, more than one Class can move. If they live inside one path, that Class moves. Time is the limit, not a tax.

## Design notes

- Working term: **the System**.
- **Deed** = a specific thing that happened. **Path** = the ongoing shape of the life.
- **Level** = investment inside a Class. **Hierarchy** = quality of the Class pool. Do not say “low Class / high Class” when you mean level.
- Canon base: mind and life, not only a résumé; Skills from a Class pool; need and desire color the pick; draws exist; ordinary voice at sleep; counter-leveling may speak while awake.
- Kit: per-pool ceiling of up to 20 levels above that Class’s current height. Beyond it, narrator only.
- Kit: off-level Skills use the Class they were living when unsure. No floating pool.
- Kit: levels grant Skills and the fact of the level, not a default stat block. Level is still a heavy multiplier.
- Kit: slow growth is lack of challenge or spent passion, not age as a law.
- Kit: multiple Classes limited by time and drive, not a fine.
- Process and examples. No exhaustive lists.

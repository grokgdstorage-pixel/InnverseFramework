# Conditions

## Scope

Application manual for **one product: a Condition**.

Read `the-system.md` first. If the mark is a Class, a Skill, or a level, leave this file.

This file answers:

- what a Condition is in this kit
- how to tell it from a Skill (including an always-on Skill)
- when one is honest
- the kinds that actually appear
- how one is lived with and how it comes off

This file does not answer:

- Class identity (`classes.md`)
- which Skill to pick (`skills.md`)
- when a level fires (`leveling.md`)
- System announcement colors (parked)
- magic
- a list of legal Conditions

## How to use

If you have not walked Watch → Judge → Product on `the-system.md`, stop and do that first.

Open this file only after the **Skill vs Condition** test below. If the honest product is a Skill, go to `skills.md`.

### Skill vs Condition

A Skill is a capability of a Class — fired, toggled, or always on. It comes from that Class’s pool. They can be offered it, refuse it, grow it, store it with the Class.

A Condition is a stamp *on the person* that is not a technique they own.

Ask: **tool of the Class, or stamp on the person?**

- Always-on from a level-up defaults to **Skill**. Do not stamp a Condition because the effect never turns off.
- New position or job → Class.
- Feeling with no stamp → `mind.md`.

If both still fit, pick **Skill** and tell the narrator.

### Output

Grok ruling packet, not forum text.

Return:

1. Outcome options: none / Condition / Skill instead / Class instead / change of the living mark / remove. Do not pick one in silence.
2. When a Condition is on the table, two lists:
   - **List A — effects.** What sits on them. Plain speech. No bracket names.
   - **List B — names.** Possible Condition names only. Do not pair 1:1 unless asked.
3. Which kind (below) and how it would come off, if known. If the lift is unknown, say so.
4. A short reason on each option.
5. If accepted: `record.md` under Conditions and other marks. Dated announce on `history-system.md`.

Narrator finalizes. Grok proposes.

### Stops

- No catalog.
- No color key.
- Do not use a Condition as a consolation prize when a Class or Skill was not “earned enough.”
- Do not invent a lift date as law.
- Example names are illustrations.
- Off-screen infliction is the narrator.
- Red / invisible marks: do not assume they can refuse them the way they refuse a Class offer.

### Required from the person

From `record.md` and the compile:

- current Classes, levels, held Skills
- Conditions already on them
- what was done to them, hung on them, or changed in the body

## What a Condition is

A System stamp on an effect that *sits on the person* — always-on or triggered — that they did not take as a tool of a Class.

It can be a gift that arrived with growth and is still not a technique (rare in this kit; prefer Skill). It can be a disease. It can be hung by someone else. It can be a Skill whose whole job was to put a mark on them.

It is not a score. It is not a mood.

## Kinds

Not a menu. Use the kind that matches what happened.

**Hung or applied.** Someone else’s Skill, vengeance, or power puts the mark on them. `[Normalize Condition]` is this door used in reverse (a Skill that strips).

**Disease / curse / infection.** A plague or contamination the Design acknowledges. May be **invisible** until it is gone.

**Red distress / Horror.** Negative stamp tied to Horror Ranks or a red Class. Do not assume it is refusable.

**Attention or body rewrite.** The world treats them differently, or the body is not what it was, and that is not a Class technique.

**With a Class step (canon blur).** The books sometimes label a standing body-effect a Condition when a Class evolves. In this kit those are **Skills** unless the narrator wants the canon label. See Design notes.

## Lived with

On `record.md`: name — what it appears to do. If it twists a Skill or Class, cite that card. Do not silently rewrite the Skill.

Other people may treat the mark as true when the bearer does not (invisible; or they refuse to believe it).

Do not level a Condition.

## How it comes off

There is no single lift menu. Match the kind:

- Disease / infection → cleanse, cure, or an expert / Skill that strips marks
- Fed by a Class or Horror Rank → may go if that Class or Rank goes
- Hung by someone → may last until the hanging is broken or a strip-Skill lands
- Unknown → say the lift is unknown. Narrator decides whether to hunt a method

Do not lift because the scene ended. Do not keep a mark when the stamp is clearly gone.

Refused: a *beneficial offer* may be refused like a Skill offer (deeds stay, offer dies). Do not assume a red or invisible mark offered a choice.

## Examples

Not a catalog.

**Always-on from a level.** They become a harder kind of [Knight] and their body stores light. That is a **Skill** in this kit. Do not stamp a Condition because it never turns off.

**Infection.** A wound leaves a metal-eating plague in them. They may not know. Condition. Comes off by cleansing, not by “they stopped using it.”

**Hung.** Someone puts their vengeance on the bearer. Condition. Not a Class called [Avenger] unless they inhabit that role.

**Attention.** A mark that only draws the wrong kind of notice. Condition-shaped, not a fame Skill they fire.

**New post.** Last defenders of a liege. If they inhabit the role → Class. If something is merely true of them (sworn, last standing, hunted) and is not the job → Condition. When unsure, Class and tell the narrator.

**Feeling.** They grieve. `mind.md`. No Condition.

**Player want.** They ask for a doom-mark after a quiet week. None.

## Design notes

- This kit prefers Skill for Class-pool passives. That is an adaptation. Canon sometimes files those as Conditions (Rabbiteater’s body lines). We do not copy that blur.
- “Conditions Met” on a leveling table means Class evolution requirements. It is not a `[Condition]` stamp. Do not use that phrase here for both.
- Colors and Horror Ranks stay parked except as doors that can feed a red Condition.
- List A / List B matches Skills. Names are not glued to effects.

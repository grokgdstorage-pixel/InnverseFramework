# How magic works

## Scope

Parent manual for **magic** in this kit.

Magic is world physics. It sits **beside** the System. It is not a fourth System product.

Cheat (do not mash these):
- **Source** — what it draws on (mana, tribe, meaning, wild). Not a Class name.
- **Manner** — Calculated or Felt. Not source. Not boxed vs freeform. Not [Wizard] vs [Sorcerer].
- **Boxed vs freeform** — System template vs shaped by the caster. Not manner.
- **Class dye** — System investment / specialization. Does not rewrite physics.
- **Range** (Quality on `magic.md`) — what they can actually hold. Not the Skill +20 ceiling.
- Named-spell List A / List B live in `spellcraft.md`.

Read `the-system.md` first if a Class, Skill, level, or Condition might move. Then return here for the magic.

This file answers:

- what magic is
- what it draws on (**source**)
- how a person casts (**manner of casting**)
- boxed spell vs freeform
- Bound Spell on a non-caster
- how magic Classes hang on that physics
- how far a named spell may go (tier as a range)

This file does not answer:

- Class identity (`classes.md`)
- which Skill to pick (`skills.md`)
- when a level fires (`leveling.md`)
- a directory of spells or magic Classes
- Wistram politics
- full manuals for witch, shaman, druid, or miracle
- artifact crafting as a trade

## How to use

Open this file when the question is magic itself: can they cast, what kind, how they cast, whether the System is boxing it, whether a spec is drifting into a new [Mage].

If the question is “do they get a new Class name,” stop and open `classes.md`.
If the question is “do they step,” open `leveling.md`.
If the question is “what capability lands,” and it is a Skill not a spell, open `skills.md`.

### Output

Grok ruling packet, not play-post text and not the archive Body.

Return:

1. Magic outcome options: no cast / boxed / freeform / Bound Spell / wrong source / wait. Do not pick one in silence. Grade of the box (crude or practiced) is a reason on the boxed option, not a separate product.
2. Source in play (living sources only: mana, tribe, meaning, wild)
3. Manner of casting if it matters: **Calculated** and/or **Felt**, with a short reason
4. If a named spell must be offered, open `spellcraft.md` and produce List A / List B there. Do not emit a second pair in this file.
5. Whether `classes.md`, `skills.md`, or `leveling.md` should open

Default: options plus a reason each. Oversupply is fine. Narrator cuts.

### Stops

- No spell directory. List A and List B are options for this scene, not a legal list of all spells.
- Named-spell List A / List B live in `spellcraft.md`. Do not emit a second pair here.
- No magic-Class directory.
- Manner of casting is not a Class name.
- Manner is not a source.
- Manner is not boxed vs freeform.
- Felt casting is not a miracle.
- Do not run witch / shaman / druid through `spellcraft.md` unless they are also walking the mana path.
- Specialization does not ban other spells.
- Do not invent a tier-to-level table.
- Do not treat practiced-vs-crude as a third named state. It is grade inside the box.
- Miracle is extinct in living memory. Do not hand it as a modern path.
- System colors: parked.

## What magic is

Magic is a principle of this world, like mass or time. It lets someone act past ordinary limits.

Mana and named spells are the common face. They are not the whole. No mana does not always mean no magic.

The System did not invent magic. It **boxes** a lot of modern spellwork so more people can use a usable template. Boxing is industrialization, not perfection.

## Source

What the magic draws on. Class names an investment in a source. It does not invent the source.

- **Mana** — internal pool, ambient, stones, potions. Default [Mage] path.
- **Tribe / shared meaning** — shaman path. Power moves with the community.
- **Emotion, place, social weight** — witch path. What people already feel about a thing.
- **Wild** — druid path. Old growth and nature-weight. Thin in living memory. Do not treat “unmake” as a required school.

Miracle is not a living source. It is an **extinct and forgotten** system. It left with the gods. Almost no one in Innverse remembers it. The few who do are rare. Living people meet it only as a scrap in the oldest history or tale. Do not offer miracle as a current Class path. Do not let a player “remember the gods” into a miracle without narrator override.

Sources overlap. Dual-walk is allowed among living sources. Do not invent a new source to please a player want.

## Manner of casting

How they take hold of the same source. Two manners. Always name both when it matters.

- **Calculated** — values, steps, systems, things that can be measured
- **Felt** — shape, pressure, wonder, inhabited rather than derived

The same [Mage] line can use either. The Magical Door is the picture: some treat the matrix as numbers; some treat the same spell as a felt shape. Neither manner is “the real [Mage].”

Do not map Calculated to [Wizard] and Felt to [Sorcerer]. Those names are other habits and prestige.

A witch may be Calculated. A Wistram [Mage] may be Felt.

## Boxed and freeform

**Boxed.** The System has standardized a named spell. Correct inputs → the System finishes a cast even if the caster does not understand the whole machine. Copied. Bulk. A **set window**: the spell can be crude or practiced, but it will not usually fall through the floor or break the ceiling of that template. That is the point. A lower-quality caster still gets a usable result. A higher-quality caster learns faster because the box carries the parts they have not mastered yet.

Crude vs practiced is **grade inside the box**, not a third product. Dull shards at Wistram are a poor grade of a boxed spell. Sharper shards after drilling are a better grade of the same box. Not freeform. Not past the box’s ceiling.

**Freeform.** They leave the factory spec and shape the effect themselves. Potential is **unrestrained in both directions**. It is not always worse. It is not always better. It follows the caster.

- Most casters are average. For them boxed is the stronger, cleaner result. Freeform is sloppy, wasteful, or fails.
- True high quality (gift, genius, real understanding of the principles): they can still use the box, and they can also go *past* it — more efficient, more precise, or a larger effect than the template allows. That is where freeform is worth more than the box.

Do not treat freeform as “the hungry bad version.” Do not treat it as automatic superiority. Judge the caster. Narrator-gated when the claimed result is past what their quality and understanding can hold.

Quality is not invented in the moment. Read the **character profile**. If the profile does not speak to gift, genius, or real grasp of principles, treat them as average. Do not invent a prodigy to justify freeform that beats the box.

Freeform is **harder and rarer** than boxed. Most people learn named spells from books, teachers, and the System. The box finishes the parts they do not understand. That ease means they never build the skill of constructing a spell or seeing the core principles. A life of boxed casting does not produce a freeform caster. Someone who only ever filled templates will usually fail if they drop the box, whatever their Class height.

Because the majority of [Mages] sit in the average range, boxed is what the world sees. Freeform that actually beats the template lives in the hands of the few high-quality casters. The practice is rare. Knowledge of it is thin. Most people never hear a true account of it.

**Bound Spell.** A non-caster receives a spell-shaped gift on a level. It behaves like a Skill (limited, often cooldown). It is not a [Mage] repertoire. `skills.md` / `leveling.md` if the gift is the question.

## How Classes hang on this

A magic Class is the System investing in a source and a focus. It does not rewrite physics.

- [Mage] and its specializations: mana source, boxed spells as default, one area of **hyperfocus**
- Hyperfocus does **not** close other spells. They can still cast outside the spec.
- A spell here and there outside the spec is fine. That is not a new Class.
- A real second course of study outside the spec starts a **new baseline [Mage] from the beginning**. The specialized Class stays. Two Classes. `classes.md` owns the names; `leveling.md` owns which height moves.
- This divergence is **mind-led**. Intention and mindset weigh more than hours. A season of fire study can do it. So can an intense weekend if they actually mean to pursue that subject as a path, not as a trick for one fight. Casual curiosity is not enough. A pile of hours with no desire to walk the new subject is not enough.
- If mind and time disagree, list both readings. Do not resolve in silence.
- [Witch], [Shaman], and [Druid] sit on other living sources. Do not run them through the mage-path teaching steps unless they are also walking the mana path.
- Mage-path teaching (learn / throw / invent a named spell) lives in `spellcraft.md`. Mana path only. Not a law of all magic.
- Miracle Classes are not a modern offer.

Most modern [Mages] live on boxed spells. The box is how average casters stay effective and how good casters learn without building every spell from nothing. That same ease is why freeform stays rare and little-known.

## Tiers

Tiers 0–9 are **ranges**, not XP.

Rough modern picture: 0–1 almost anyone on the mana path; 2 takes study; 3 is the usual bar for most; 4–6 is uncommon height; 7+ is rare in the present and hungry; 9 can kill the caster.

The same named spell changes size with talent and pool. A low [Druid] and an [Archmage] can both “grow grass.” The output is not the same.

Lower height can punch a higher tier with practice and time. Do not print a table that says Tier N requires Level X.

Treat “past this range” like the Skill ceiling: Grok stays in-range; narrator only beyond it.

## Examples

Not a catalog.

**Packet shape.** Someone on a mana path wants an effect at the door.
Options: boxed (say whether the grade is crude or practiced) / no cast / freeform if their quality and understanding could hold it (better *or* worse than the box).
List A (effects): move a body across the linked space; spend the door’s stored charge; fail and dump the charge.
List B (names): proposed titles only, not glued to the lines above.
Manner: Calculated, Felt, or both listed. Not a new Class because they studied one evening.

**Same Class, two manners.** Several [Mage]-line people on one artifact. Some measure cost and matrix. Some inhabit the shape. Both are honest. Do not rename anyone [Sorcerer] for feeling it.

**Dull shards.** Student has the named ice/stone package. Output is blunt. Instructor drills the fill. Still boxed. Better grade. Not freeform. Not a spec change.

**High height, no principles.** A boxed caster with real Class height drops the template and tries to invent the effect. Options: fail / weak freeform / stay in the box. Height alone does not grant freeform.

**Spec, still other spells.** [Cryomancer] throws a small fire spell they already know. Allowed. They are not stripped of ice.

**Spec, real drift.** [Cryomancer] takes up fire as a path they mean to walk — a season of study, or an intense weekend with real desire to pursue it. Options: stay spec-only (dishonest if the intent was real) / keep [Cryomancer] and open a new baseline [Mage] at low height. `classes.md` + `leveling.md`. Hours without that mindset: not a new Class.

**Wrong source.** They want shaman-weight with no tribe and no shared meaning. No cast, or a weak mana substitute if they actually have that path. Do not invent tribe-power.

**Miracle want.** They ask to call on forgotten gods. Default: no. Ancient tale, not a living path. Narrator only.

**Player want.** They ask for a Tier 7 spell after a quiet week of boxed Tier 2. In-range options only.

## Design notes

- Magic is beside the System. Boxed is a window, not the whole art. Freeform can undershoot or overshoot that window. Quality of the caster decides.
- Most casters are average, so boxed is the common art. Freeform that beats the box is rare and little-known.
- Quality comes from the character profile. Grok does not invent a prodigy.
- Source ≠ manner ≠ Class ≠ boxed/freeform.
- Calculated / Felt are the only two manners this file names.
- Crude vs practiced is grade inside boxed, not a third state.
- Specialization = hyperfocus. Drift = new baseline [Mage] plus the spec that remains. Mind-led: intent to walk the new subject matters more than a clock.
- Miracle is extinct with the gods. Mention only.
- Process and examples. No spell list.
